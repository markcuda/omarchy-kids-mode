# Small Wonders

An Omarchy Kids theme. Design direction: ages 5–8; available to everyone.

A welcoming Miasma adaptation with smoky charcoal, linen text, mossy olive, fern green, copper, and butterfly amber. It preserves Miasma’s olive blue slot, copper magenta, and amber cyan, lifting readability rather than replacing its earthy character with pastels.

The palette is in `colors.toml`; wallpapers are in `backgrounds/`. A matching Base24 export is included. Text and accent roles meet 4.5:1 against base and panel backgrounds in static checks.


See `ATTRIBUTION.md` for creator credits and wallpaper source links.
