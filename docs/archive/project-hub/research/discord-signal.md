## From Harris Kenny [OMA] (Sept 2026)

Two bigger projects people are coalescing around:

1. **Onboarding flow and parent/child setup**
2. **Mascot for onboarding** — "with the concept of a boy/girl/neutral set of paths proposed.
   (as a father of a girl and a boy and seeing differences between them, this makes sense to me)"

For lone wolves: "themes and plugins that can be pre-packaged, make this on your own and make
something cool and fun! so when they log in, they have something to do 🙂"

Two more ways in: "just download omarchy on an old laptop and build something for the kid in your
life (or yourself, we have a 14yo in here)" — and [register for an Omarchy meetup](https://omarchy.org/meetups/)
and invite other parents and caregivers.

Earlier framing: "Omarchy upstream and independent features, themes, plugins, etc. for children
with a focus around child safety, fun, learning, etc. Kids deserve awesome computers too!"
